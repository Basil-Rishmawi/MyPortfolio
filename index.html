About Me

I'm a third-year Software Engineering student at Bethlehem University. I like building things that work in the real world, from Java applications and network designs to small electronic games on Arduino. I care about understanding how systems fit together, from the database design to the network underneath.

Music is the other half of who I am. I studied Oud at the Edward Said National Conservatory of Music, and I also received a scholarship to learn Buzuq. Both worlds teach me the same things: patience, practice, and attention to detail. I'm currently looking for opportunities to grow as a developer, through internships, team projects, or anything that lets me combine technology and music.
